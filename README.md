# Hey, I am Raffael

I live in Basel and work as an AI architect. By day I am Enterprise Solution Architect for AI Platforms and
Agentic AI at [Helvetia Baloise Group](https://www.helvetiabaloise.com/), helping define a Group-wide platform
for the secure, governed use of foundation models, agents, and AI-assisted engineering.

Outside of work I build automation-first platforms, edge systems, and developer tooling that survive production
reality, mostly under [raskell.io](https://www.raskell.io) (raskell = rascal × Haskell × Raffael, tanuki mascot
included). Tech that serves humans. Boring by default, precise when required.

## What I work on

- **AI and agent platforms:** model gateways, orchestration, context, memory, tool use, governance, observability
- **Platform automation:** workflows, guardrails, self-service, operability
- **Edge systems:** reverse proxies, policy, observability, safe change
- **Applied security:** reduce noise, keep humans effective, ship pragmatic controls
- **Open standards:** sustainable solutions over vendor lock-in
