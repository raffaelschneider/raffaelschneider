# Hey, I’m Raffael 🌿

I build **automation-first platforms** and **edge/web ingress systems** that survive production reality — and I now do the same for **enterprise AI platforms**: governed foundation models, agentic systems, and the architecture that holds them together.
Applied security is one arena of the same problem: **systems under pressure, adversarial inputs, and messy constraints**.

- Enterprise Solution Architect — AI Platforms & Agentic AI at [Helvetia Baloise Group](https://www.helvetiabaloise.com/)
- Founder of [Archipelag.io](https://archipelag.io)
- Based in Basel, Switzerland
- Writing: https://raskell.io
- Speaking: [talks and slides](https://raskell.io/speaking/)

## What I work on
- Enterprise AI platforms (model gateways, agent orchestration, context/memory/tool-use architecture)
- AI governance at scale (security, observability, compliance, auditability, adoption roadmaps)
- Platform automation (workflows, guardrails, self-service, operability)
- Edge systems (reverse proxies, policy, observability, safe change)
- Applied security (signal > noise, pragmatic controls, incident-driven improvements)
- Preference: **open standards and sustainable solutions over vendor lock-in**
